# Deviations from the original

Every intentional difference between the remake and the original, with its reason. The specs in `docs/spec/` always describe the original; replay and screen comparisons mask or skip these. Port-menu improvements are not listed: with them off, the game behaves as below.

| Area | Original | Remake | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overrunning passes | When a frame's logic is still running at the next frame, the rest of it is dropped (`game-flow.md`) | Every logic step completes | Hardware limit (CPU time) |
| Sprites | Flicker and the 8-sprites-per-line limit | All sprites drawn every frame, no flicker | Hardware limit |
| Tow beam order | The sprite table is built in ascending slot order on one step and descending on the next, so the beam (slot 2) is in front of the towed item (slots 11-14) on half the steps (`player.md` 8.4, `objects.md` 6.6) | The beam is drawn behind every object as well as the pod, so the rope always meets the item it holds | Hardware limit (sprite rotation against flicker) |
| Display refresh | HUD, score and other screen updates run as rotating jobs, one per frame, to fit video-memory bandwidth (`game-flow.md`) | Refreshed every frame. Animation and palette-cycling speeds are design and keep their timing | Hardware limit (video-memory bandwidth) |
| Scrolling | Seams and attribute glitches at the edges while scrolling | Drawn straight from the map, no seams | Hardware limit |
| Passwords | A hand-made password with an impossible level number (13-63) is accepted (`password.md`) | Rejected as invalid | Bug |
| Loading frames | A scene change spends a variable number of frames decoding data, during which the frame counter keeps advancing; scenes that keep the counter then feed it to the random generator (`game-flow.md` 1.1, 5, 8) | Scene changes are instant, so the frame counter and random sequence can differ after one. Replays re-sync the frame counter and random state from the original at each scene change | Hardware limit (CPU time) |
| Item spawn churn | Where a level's despawn margin is narrower than the items' spawn window, an item just off-screen is spawned and freed again every few steps, holding a slot part of the time (`objects.md` 3.5, `levels.md` 3.3) | Items are spawned only inside the despawn window, so none churns | Bug (two windows disagree); never visible |
| Push while protected | While the pod is protected after emerging or respawning (RAM $97), an overlapping object's push leaves an erratic leftover speed (`behaviour.md` 3.1, Q11) | The pod's speed on that axis is set to 0 | Bug (stale value) |
| Waypoint patrols | Type 21's "waypoint reached" test also accepts a target 256-271 px below, so on level 2 one creature never reaches its second waypoint and turns back about 300 px early (`behaviour.md`, quirk Q1) | The test compares the full distance, so the creature patrols the whole route in the level data | Bug: the intended route is in the data |
| Tow lift sign | Whether the rope's pull adds to or takes from the pod's lift comes from a byte left over from an earlier multiplication (bit 0 of an object-table byte), not from the pull, so it flips about 1 step in 8 at high swing speed with thrust (`player.md` 8.3) | The lift takes the sign of the rope's actual pull. Replays validate with the original rule, and unit tests cover the remake's rule | Bug |
| Sprites at the screen's edges | A sprite's y wraps round: one partly above the top is hidden whole, and one just above it can show at the bottom (flight's stars, the launch pod's nose) | Drawn where it belongs, its visible part at the top and nothing at the bottom | Hardware limit |
| Sprites behind the scenery | A sprite behind the background shows through every transparent (colour 0) pixel of it, so the mothership landing behind a cliff shows through the cliff's black outlines and dark holes (`scenes.md` 8.1) | In the surface scene, transparent pixels next to the scenery's colours or in its small holes (up to 4 px either way) hide the sprite as well, so the ship is hidden by the whole cliff | Hardware limit (sprite priority per pixel) |
| Colour cells | Colours are chosen per 2 × 2 tiles, so each row of the planet menu box (and of its wipe at blast-off) recolours the picture's row next to it until the box covers it: the rock under the half-drawn box flashes blue and white (`scenes.md` 8, "Colours") | The box's own cells take the box's palette; the picture keeps its colours | Hardware limit (attribute grid) |
| Surface scene's bottom lines | The scroll of 2 shows the picture's top row again on lines 238-239, which a television's overscan hides | Lines 238-239 show the backdrop colour | Hardware limit (overscan) |
| Drawing between steps | Each frame shows one pass (the remake draws faster displays by blending two passes) | The pod and any moving object are placed by their blended offset from the blended camera, then rounded; rounding each on its own would put them a pixel back and forth on the screen while the camera follows | Port (display rate) |
| Title stars | One star of the title's 56-star table is drawn at y 160 instead of 169 (`scenes.md` 2.1) | Drawn at 169 | Bug |
| Title song | Song 1 plays once (2,662 frames), so the rest of the title and the attract cycle are silent (`scenes.md` 15) | It starts again whenever it ends, until another song starts | Port |
| Attract demo sound | The demo starts no level song and drops every sound request (`scenes.md` 3.1, `game-flow.md` 7.1) | It plays the level's song and its effects. `replay-compare` runs the demos silent | Port |
| Attract demo pilot | An autopilot steers the pod along a fixed list of waypoints per demo, blind to the terrain and the enemies, until the pod is destroyed (`game-flow.md` 7.1) | A rules-based pilot (`src/game/pilot`) plays from the engine's state, in the engine chosen: it finds a path through the level's terrain to an item, tows it home, and shoots or shields on the way. The demo ends on the delivery (or any other scene change), the pod's loss, a button or after two minutes. Levels, equipment and weapons are the original's. `replay-compare --demos` still flies the original's autopilot | Port (the waypoint lists break on any change to the levels or the physics, and the Scaled engine leaves their path at once) |

| Planet map wrap | On a planet narrower than the map window, the window's rows run on into the next map row, so planet 0 shows every row twice (`scenes.md` 13) | Each map row is shown once; the window beyond the planet's edge is empty | Bug |
| Aimed shots near level (Q12) | An aimed shot's vertical speed drops its sign; when the random offset outweighs a small \|dy\| it reads as about 256 px per step and the shot vanishes (`behaviour.md` 2.8) | The vertical speed is signed, so the shot drifts slightly the other way, as it does horizontally | Bug |
| Missile steering (Q13) | The straight-or-diagonal test is inverted on the vertical axis, so a missile above or below its target zig-zags (`behaviour.md` 2.9) | The horizontal rule applies on both axes | Bug |
| Type 22 volley (Q15) | The four shots overwrite slots 15-18 whether or not they are in use, and the two down-going shots get 1.84 / 3.004 instead of 0.84 / 3.00 (`behaviour.md` 4.11) | Shots go only into free slots among 15-18 (any that do not fit are not fired), and all four have the up-going shots' speeds | Bug |
| Lifted dome speed (Q14) | Moves twice per step while homing on the pod (`behaviour.md` 4.7) | Moves once per step | Bug |
| Indestructible obstacles (Q16) | The "no damage" flag blocks only the pod's touch, so shots wear down walkers, pillars and the slot 24-25 flames (255 hit points) (`behaviour.md` 3.5) | Objects with the flag take no damage from shots either | Bug |
| Stale slot fields (Q17, also Q30, Q31, Q41) | A reused slot keeps some of the previous occupant's fields: sub-pixel position, work and speed bytes, the fire timer (`behaviour.md` 2.5) | Every created object starts from cleared fields plus what its creator sets | Bug |
| Dome pull (Q18) | The type 11 dome flips the pod's vertical direction and moves it \|dy\|, which lifts a descending pod onto its row but would send a rising pod away from it (`behaviour.md` 4.7) | The pod is always moved \|dy\| towards the dome's row, heading towards it | Bug |
| Pod-below flag at range (Q19) | The "pod below the object" flag is left 0 when \|dx\| ≥ 256, so a distant object treats the pod as above or level whatever their heights (`behaviour.md` 2.4) | The flag is computed at any distance | Bug (a case of Q17) |
| Final-level piece homing (Q20) | A state $21 piece skips its homing on the step its counter is 8 (`behaviour.md` 4.16) | It homes on every step of its first 32 | Bug (a case of Q17) |
| Final-level piece life (Q21) | Pieces from a shot level-26 object keep the slot's old counter, so they live 82-128 steps and may not home (`behaviour.md` 4.16) | The counter starts at 0: 128 steps of life, homing for the first 32 | Bug (a case of Q17) |
| Star marker by touch (Q22) | A star collected by touch makes a marker that is freed before it shows; a shot star's marker rises and shows (`behaviour.md` Q22) | Both show the rising marker | Bug |
| Item pull-back (Q23) | With slots 11 and 12 occupied, the item in slot 14 (or 13 while the beam holds 14) creeps 1 px per axis per step back to its level position, and its list entry is reset (`behaviour.md` 6.1, Q23) | Items stay where they are | Hardware limit: likely keeps the sprite count down |
| Distance at long range (Q24) | When the distance is capped at 255 the routine leaves \|dy\| unfinished (a leftover byte, or the low byte of the difference), so far-off drones and hoppers act on a junk value (`behaviour.md` 2.4) | \|dy\| is always computed, capped at 255 | Bug (as Q19) |
| Green creature pause (Q25) | A repeated vertical choice counts as "same mode" only once; the next one starts a 16-step pause (`behaviour.md` 4.13) | A repeated vertical choice continues the mode, as a horizontal one does | Bug |
| Fuel pod refill (Q26) | Sets only the fuel's high byte, keeping the old low byte (`behaviour.md` 3.3) | Fuel is set to exactly $E000 | Bug; never visible |

| Missile with no target (Q27) | A pod missile with no target stores slot 0, which is the pod, so it turns back and circles its owner (`behaviour.md` 5.11.1) | With no target it keeps its current direction until it finds one | Bug |

## Validation mode

Every deviation above that changes the game state (edge spawn wrap, the warp mesh on 37 and 39, the wormhole's sparkle slot, the warp-level intro length, push while protected, waypoint patrols, tow lift sign, aimed shots, missile steering, the type 22 volley, the lifted dome's speed, shots against obstacles, stale slot fields, the dome's pull, a pod missile with no target, star-bullet children, the bombs' explosions and the parallax stars 3, 5 and 7) runs through one core-internal switch, `Gameplay::set_original_rules`. It is off in play and in the attract demos, and on in `replay-compare`, which then validates the original's rules (also for the original's demos, whose autopilot steers blind, so the fixed enemies would lead it off the original's path within half a minute). (The original's table lookup on levels 37 and 39 is a black-box fit of one run, not the real register; no replay validates it.) It is not a port-menu switch. A new state-changing deviation checks the same flag and gets a unit test for the remake's rule and, where it exists, the original's.

The scenes' deviations that change what is shown or the state (Q34, Q35, Q36, Q37, Q42 and Q43, the credits' line 232, and the stale slot fields of the bonus flight's objects, Q17) follow the same switch in `Game::set_original_rules`; `sj-tools scene --compare` turns it on and `--remake` turns it off.

## Kept on purpose

Quirks that look unintended but stay as in the original.

- **Tow swing speed (`player.md` 8.3):** the swing speed's cap is supposed to hold W at 1.00, but it only trips on a carry, so W climbs to just under 2.00 and then drops back. Kept, because it is part of how towing feels.
- **Demo autopilot (`game-flow.md` 7.1):** where the original's demos are replayed (`replay-compare --demos`), the autopilot's steering quirks are kept, so it flies the same path as the original's.
- **Left-column mask (`graphics.md` 1.3):** the original blanks the left 8 background pixels on every scene's main picture (the flight and launch screens also blank the sprites there). Kept, so every screen has the original's framing; the remake has no seams to hide there anyway.
- **Pod facing across level changes (Q44, `behaviour.md`):** reset after a wormhole gate, kept after the warp-level countdown. Kept: no intent is evident and it is harmless.

## The Scaled engine

The Scaled engine (`docs/scaled.md`; PHYSICS: REMASTERED, the default) plays the rules above with its physics at the display's rate. With PHYSICS: ORIGINAL the game is the 30 Hz step above. These differences of play are inside `sj-tools scaled-compare`'s budget; none is a setting.

| Area | Original | Scaled | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Input | Read once a step: a press waits up to 33 ms, and a tap between two reads is lost | Read every tick (at most 1/R late); a press between ticks is kept, so a tap always turns, fires or switches once | Responsiveness |
| Thrust | Acts one step after B (the step's move comes first) | Acts in the tick that sees B; the pod then leads the Original's path by up to one step of its speed | Responsiveness |
| Thrust carry-in | The horizontal carry-in byte is whatever the star job left; the vertical cap is 3.00 | The horizontal carry-in is 0; the vertical cap is lowered by the part of the step's gravity still to come, which gives the Original's speed at the cap | Rate-free rules |
| Terrain contact | Tested once a step, which a fast pod can pass by a thin wall's corner | Tested every tick, so a contact comes up to a step sooner and nothing is passed; a slide along a slope makes 7-29% fewer sounds and sparks; a deep wall hit halves the speed once; bounce timing leads by 30-40 ms after a few bounces | Rate-free contact |
| Pod against objects | Tested once a step | Tested every tick, each object at most once a step of game time; a fast object is noticed when the step would have passed it | Rate-free contact |
| Homing enemies | Their 30 Hz decisions | The same decisions, made a step ahead against the pod a step ahead, shown on their way between steps; their paths drift up to about 10 px in chaotic stretches | Smooth motion |
| Camera | The -9 quirk of the half-step clamp | A symmetric clamp (the quirk's moves never occur in play) | Glitch |
| Stars | Half-rate star job when the terrain lists are long | The stars follow every camera move | Glitch |
| Tow | A thrust without a load turns W on a leftover byte, kept for the next catch | No torque without a load; a catch starts W at 0 | Glitch (stale byte) |
| Gate | The counter's forced nibble at the gate's entry | The gate's cadences from its own start | Rate-free cadences |

The pod rest is an addition, not a deviation, and comes with PHYSICS: REMASTERED: an upright pod landing on flat ground slower than 2.00 px per step comes to rest instead of hopping.
