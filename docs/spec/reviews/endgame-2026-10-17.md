Reviewer: clean-room reviewer, `docs/` only (nothing from the analysts' workspace read). | Build | Verdict |

## Blocking gaps

1. **Where the pod appears after a level change is contradictory or missing.** `game-flow.md` 7.4 (code 3, reload table) says the player position is kept, with "camera y and player y go down by 128" when landing on a planet. `levels.md` Appendix A says the entry position is "header bytes 0-7 of level L". The kind-18 wormhole stands at the second level's home position (`objects.md` 10), which fits "header home", not "kept". Nothing states where the pod lands on planet p when it returns from the second level (the planet's wormhole? kept x,y minus 128?) or from a warp level. There is no worked example for 13+p to p or 29+p to p. Missing for each arrival: position, camera, speeds, direction and facing, fuel, and the state after the gate (`$97` = 64 is named but its effect is only in `player.md` 5.4).
   *Sections: `game-flow.md` 7.4 "Code 3" and "Reload"; `levels.md` 3 header table and Appendix A; `objects.md` 10; `economy.md` 5.3.*

2. **When the gate state starts: two specs say opposite things.** `player.md` 3 transitions table: "any to 9 ... while the transition guard *refuses* transition 3". `player.md` 9.2, `game-flow.md` 7.4 (guard) and 9.1 say the gate state starts when the guard *lets the request pass*, and that a refused touch is ignored. The guard matters in play: the pod arrives on the second level's wormhole, so the first 64 steps are always refused. Also unspecified: whether a refused touch on a planet still bounces, and whether a second level's wormhole bounces at all (`economy.md` 5.3 says no fuel condition on levels 13-41).
   *Sections: `player.md` 3, 9.2; `game-flow.md` 7.4, 9.1.*

3. **The wormhole touch itself is only summarised, and mostly [C].** `economy.md` 5.3 says the box is 13 px on both axes (centre not stated) and that a pod with an item or probe shields does nothing. Missing:
   - Whether Jetman alone can enter. `sound.md` 8.1 says "the pod or Jetman"; `economy.md` 5.3 and `objects.md` 10 say pod only. `player.md` 9.2 keeps "the previous state" without saying which are allowed.
   - Where in the pod's step the test runs.
   - The bounce. `economy.md` 5.3 says "`player.md` owning the rest of the motion", but `player.md` has no wormhole-bounce section. Missing: the sparkle's slot, position, life and frames (the "slot 3" of `objects.md` 10 against the sparkle ring $69-$6C of 7), the "pod flash", the direction-bit flip and sound $44 timing, and whether the hit pause `$99` or invulnerability is set.
   *Sections: `economy.md` 5.3; `objects.md` 10; `sound.md` 8.1, 8.3; `player.md` 9.2.*

4. **The gate animation (state 9) is not a rule an implementer can follow.** 9.2 says "every object slot is moved onto the gate, particles spin round it, the player is held at it". Missing:
   - How fast the pod and each object reach the gate (instant or eased).
   - Which "particles", how many, and their radius and angular speed.
   - Whether the pod sprite is drawn, and the `$98` flash cadence.
   - Whether enemies, fuel use, gravity and damage run during the 96 counts (384 frames).
   - Whether pause is allowed (the pause table says no).
   - What the counter does at 96 other than the exit. Song 2 lasts 389 frames against 384 for the gate, so 5 frames overlap into the next screen.
   *Section: `player.md` 9.2, [C].*

5. **The warp level's world is under-specified.**
   - **Collision and bounds:** `graphics.md` 5.7.1 says "whether those blocks are visible is not known", and `economy.md` 5.4 says terrain damage is off. Nothing says whether the pod collides with the 6x6-chunk map's blocks at all (bounce, walls, stop), what the camera limits are, or what stops the pod leaving the map. The map is at bank 3 $B9DE, and its block set and chunk palettes for these levels are not described.
   - **Helper on levels 33, 35, 37 and 39:** `graphics.md` 5.7.2 sends the reader to `objects.md` for the "homing helper", but `objects.md` has no such rule. This drives the mesh drift on 4 of 13 levels.
   - **The "alternative HUD mode" ($9B bit 7):** `game-flow.md` 9 [C] and `economy.md` 5.4 name it without saying what it changes. Is the crystal count shown? Do the fuel and weapon gauges stay? `scenes.md` 10 says the gameplay status panel stays for kind 6, `scenes.md` 10.1 and `game-flow.md` 7.4 say there is no panel (gap 12).
   - **Crystals:** positions are only in the items list (to be imported), which is fine. The spawn window and four-slot rule come from `levels.md` 3.3. Not stated: the score effect of a touch (none?), the touch centre for Jetman, and what Jetman does on a warp level (can he be out of the pod?).
   - **Countdown start:** `behaviour.md` 6.3 gives 704 steps, but not when counter 0 is created (level start? first step?) or where the digit is drawn on screen (56 px below the pod).
   *Sections: `levels.md` 3.1, 6; `graphics.md` 5.7; `economy.md` 5.4; `objects.md` 7; `behaviour.md` 6.3.*

6. **Entry to the final level after the last warpship piece is one [C] line.** `economy.md` 5.5 says "sets lives, jetpods, `$D1` to 0, `$9A` = $80, `$3B` = 26, `$E0` = 0. It then launches from the mothership." `game-flow.md` 7.4 lists "the final level" as item 3 of "what follows a message screen". Not specified:
   - Which scenes run (flight? approach? surface? launch scene, with no mothership on a level with no home?).
   - Pod type, fuel and shield state, equipment kept or cleared, start position (header home x 96, y 1024) and facing.
   - Blank frames between the kind-5 message and the first frame.
   - The HUD blanking ("first frame of the final level", `game-flow.md` 1.2 table).
   - Music (song 33, from `sound.md`).
   The last delivery happens in a cavern (a ring on level 13-25), so "launch from the mothership" cannot be literal.
   *Sections: `economy.md` 5.5; `game-flow.md` 7.4, 6; `scenes.md` 9; `player.md` 9.1.*

## Contradictions and gaps that need a decision (blocking for exactness)

7. **Kind-6 screen length depends on the gameplay frame counter, with no decision.** The screen is installed by the gate's end, not by a reload, so `$5D` holds the gameplay count (random phase). Length: 256 frames if `$5D` >= 64 at the start, else 255 - s. Q33 (`behaviour.md`) and `deviations.md` fix the final message to 319, but the kind-6 screen has no quirk entry and no deviation. Decide: fixed 256? The button-skip rule ("once `$DA` is below 64") is [C] only. Also, the remake has no load frames, so it cannot reproduce `$5D` anyway.
   *Sections: `game-flow.md` 7.4 "Kind 6"; `scenes.md` 10.1; `deviations.md`.*

8. **How message 23 is drawn contradicts the catalogue.** `scenes.md` 14.1 and `game-flow.md` 7.4 say it is typed at one character per 4 frames. `text.md` 3 shows message 23 with no typewriter marker ({TYPE}), unlike 21, 22, 47 and 48, which would make it immediate. Its data (152 bytes, about 130 characters) at one per 4 frames would take over 500 frames, but `scenes.md` 14.1 says all text is on screen before frame 150. State one rule: immediate, or typed (rate, click sound $3A, per line).
   *Sections: `scenes.md` 14.1; `text.md` 2.3, 3 row 23; `game-flow.md` 7.4.*

9. **The final message length is stated four ways.**
   - `economy.md` 5.5: "255 frames".
   - `game-flow.md` 7.4 table: `$DA` = 255.
   - `game-flow.md` 7.4 text: 319 frames.
   - `scenes.md` 14.1 and Q33: 319 to 380 (+16 blank frames).
   `deviations.md` says always 319. Needed: the remake's rule in one place, including when a button is accepted (frames 256 onward, ending two frames after the press) and whether the 16 blank frames are kept. Fix `economy.md` 5.5.
   *Sections: `economy.md` 5.5; `game-flow.md` 7.4; `scenes.md` 14.1; `behaviour.md` Q33.*

10. **Arrival-to-departure behaviour of the guard on second levels is not stated for the remake.** `game-flow.md` 7.4 gives the 64-step guard but says "some behaviours clear `$41` ... or set it back to 64 [C]" without naming them. Which? An implementer cannot tell whether the pod can leave the second level sooner.

11. **Badge intermission after a warp level: where it ends.** `economy.md` 5.4 / `game-flow.md` 7.4 say "code 3 with `$DB` = 0", and code 3 from 29+ goes to `$3B` - 29. The planet-flag test is on `$3C` (the old level), but the second-level flag test in `levels.md` Appendix A needs the flag set by hand. Spell out that the return from a warp level never tests a flag and always lands on planet p (see gap 1 for the position).

## Contradictions and smaller gaps

20. Game completion has no spec for a game that reaches the final level by a password with all 12 flags set, or for planet 12 (needs 11's piece, `game-flow.md` 6 reasoning). `game-flow.md` 6 says "(reasoning from the rules above)", not observed. Mark as [H] or test it.
