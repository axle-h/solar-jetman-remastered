# Review: scenes.md (draft) with game-flow.md 7.1, 7.3, 7.4, 9.2 -- 2026-10-17

Reviewer: clean-room reviewer, docs only. Read: CLAUDE.md, CLEAN_ROOM.md, spec README, scenes.md, game-flow.md 7 and 9.2.
Mesen was not run; every point below comes from reading the specs against each other.

## Verdict

**Not approved as complete; approved with conditions for the parts that are written.**

- **Hygiene: pass with two conditions.** No assembly mnemonics and no decompiler identifiers. The demo autopilot (7.1) is the
  one place that sits close to a routine-by-routine translation (gaps 14, 15).
- **Implementability:** the title menu, password screen, approach screen, kind-6 warp screen, surface menu (layouts, modes,
  automatic blast-off) and the attract-demo rules are buildable. The licence screen, story, badge and game-over mostly are.
  An implementer **cannot** build the interplanetary flight, the map, the ending and credits, the backdrop interlude, or the
  surface mothership paths, and several scenes lack motion, palette or music detail. Since this is a draft and the file says so,
  the verdict is "draft accepted, gaps 1-8 block sign-off".

## Gaps (blocking first)

### Blocking

1. **scenes.md 5 (flight).** Only the mothership's tile block and star tiles are given. No scroll speed or path for the
   mothership, no star count, positions, speeds or spawn rule ("[H] not analysed"), no palette preset, no fade timing, no
   music. A 1,343-frame scene cannot be built.
2. **scenes.md 8 and game-flow 7.3 (first-arrival descent).** The path is "[C]" and depends on the planet picture (13 durations
   given, no paths). No start point, velocity, per-picture waypoints, sprite frames or the effect of A ("or less if A is
   pressed": how much less?). Same for the leaving flight (mode 1: exhaust sprites, path, music 5 timing) and the mode 4
   counter ($0080 to $0260 is 480 steps, yet measured 396-581 frames: how do the two relate? wipe direction [H]).
3. **scenes.md 11 (backdrop interlude, code 7).** "A few objects", no timing, no object list, no positions, no palette, no
   hand-over to the badge. The badge intermission's 255 frames are in game-flow 7.4 but scenes.md 11 still says timings are
   unanalysed: cross-reference missing, and its palette/position are not tied to 2.4's preset 20 for this use.
4. **scenes.md 13, 14 (map, ending, credits).** Not analysed. The ending and credits are part of "every scene".
5. **scenes.md 9 and game-flow 7.3 (launch scene).** 358 frames, but the pod's rise, the hatch/door timing and which sprite
   changes when are not given; only static x/y ranges. The button skip is [C].
6. **scenes.md 6 (shop and equip list).** Layout only. Missing: the horizontal scroll step and speed when the selection
   changes, cursor/selection sprite, the item box picture per item, how the count and money are formatted, repeat/auto-repeat
   of Left/Right (game-flow 3.2 is not linked), A/B behaviour, equip-list differences, the sprite-0 split value (line 44 is
   given but not what is scrolled where and by how much), and the exit transition.
7. **scenes.md 10 (message screens).** The item picture "rows 9-13 centred" has no tile mapping per item kind or per
   message, palette, and nothing says which background tile numbers hold it (sprite tiles drawn as background: from which
   table?). Message position (rows 15-17) is given for one message only; line breaks of the others are in text.md only if
   the implementer finds them.
8. **No music/sound per scene.** scenes.md never says which song plays on the licence, title, story, password, flight, shop,
   approach, game over or credits screens (only the demo is covered, and "the level music" is not identified). game-flow 7.3
   mentions music 3 and 5 on the surface only. Needed for every scene.

### Non-blocking but needed for a faithful build

9. **scenes.md 2.1, star table.** The stars take y from `bank 0:$82D1 + 4n`, x from `$82D2 + 4n`, tile from `$80D3 + 4n`. A
   stride of 4 with offsets 1, 2 and (suspiciously) another page for the tile reads as the operand bytes of consecutive
   instructions, i.e. program code, not a table; the tile address in a different page is probably a typo. Tabulate the 56
   positions and tiles in the spec instead of pointing into code; this is also a hygiene point (it asks the implementer to
   read program bytes). Also unspecified: how "one star chosen from the random number" maps to an index (mask? modulo 56?)
   and which of the 16-frame ticks resets earlier stars' palette.
10. **scenes.md 2.1 and 3 (colours).** The spec lists raw palette values ($38/$30, $07/$17, backdrop $01) and "background
    colours 13 and 15" (palette entry numbers? which of the two pairs go together? ambiguous). `CLEAN_ROOM.md` rule 5 puts colours
    in the ripped category. Replace with a reference to the palette data in `graphics.md` 7.x or the preset number.
11. **scenes.md 2.2-2.3 (title).** The homing motion that gives the camera swing is "[C] mechanism"; the implementer is
    told only the end values. Walking Jetman: his speed, animation frames and their timing, the second (palette 1) sprite's
    offset, and the cursor's offset for CONTINUE ("[C]") are not given; "about 990 frames" is not tied to a speed and distance.
12. **scenes.md 3 vs game-flow 7.1 (story).** scenes.md uses frames 318, 598, 610, 643, 700, 757; game-flow gives thresholds
    240, 320, 600, 680 and "about 755". Neither maps the other. Also missing: the frame at which the x scroll speed changes from
    2 to 1, the y wave as a formula (only "about 100 frames", range 184-202), the star sprites' positions/count/palette
    assignment, and the "faster and faster" rule for the final scroll. "Frame 4 is never shown" is not explained (is it a
    deliberate skip?).
13. **Contradiction, scenes.md 10.1 vs game-flow 7.4 (kind 6).** scenes.md says "No sprites are visible"; game-flow says an
    object in slot 11 stands at x = 128, y = 160 (frame $9E). Resolve (the open-questions list in game-flow admits what it
    shows is unknown). Also the 254-frame sample does not match the counting rule: from $5D = 172, 84 counting frames
    (172-255), 64 idle (0-63), then 108 more is 256, not 254. Say whether $DA is decremented before or after the check, or
    whether the first frame is not counted.
14. **game-flow 7.1 autopilot, hygiene.** The "Each A pass" block and `octant()` are a step-by-step algorithm including
    named restart points, an acknowledged 8-bit borrow bug ("the borrow ... is not carried"; "drop a carry"; faster()'s
    "second test sets its result before looking at the thrust"). That is a transliteration of the routine's flow and its
    flaws, not a behavioural description. The README convention asks for "what happens". Condition: state the observable rule
    (target classification into horizontal/vertical/diagonal sectors with the measured thresholds, brake vs cruise, thrust
    cut-offs) and, if exact reproduction is wanted, give the verified outputs as test vectors (the 10,869-pass comparison). PLAN.md
    Fidelity says bugs are dropped; the demo reproducing a bug needs a deviations.md entry or removal.
15. **game-flow 9.2 and 7.1, hygiene (minor).** Many CPU address ranges of code ("bank 0 $A6B6-$A71B", "$C85E-$C874",
    "$BED7, $CD99", "bank 1 $8CEC") are given as "written by/read by". Allowed as provenance, but together with the
    "A-pass item 9" labels they form a call map of the program; thin to one address per claim or to RAM facts only.
16. **game-flow 7.4 structure.** After the kind-6 section, a generic "Timing: typed text ... 64 frames ... Then: kind 6: code
    3; kind 7: the ending ..." list follows with no heading, so it reads as part of kind 6. Restore a heading for the
    general message-screen timing and exit rules. Also 7.4 "$DA: 240, or 255 for the ending" vs kind 6 = 192 and badge
    255-frame rule: summarise all in one table (kind, $DA, text, picture, next).
17. **game-flow 9.2, $98.** "for a pod after $0317 = 1 only if this planet's flag is set" is ambiguous (the same rule is
    worded as "when the player state is not 1" in 7.4). Use one wording.
18. **scenes.md 3.1 (demo).** The status panel values are stated ("score 001000"), but the demo's music (which level
    song), the lack of any fade/transition into the demo and the exact frame the title restarts after the demo (16 frames
    after state 2: in game-flow, not here) should be restated. The 7.1 claim that the pod is shielded "whenever the
    autopilot raises the shield" depends on the probe shields in $DC = $C0: say so.
19. **scenes.md 8 (surface menu).** Menu-box position for layouts 0-3 (does the box height change with 2 vs 3 options?) is
    an open question; cursor sprite, its motion and the input repeat are absent; the number of rows differs by layout. Required
    to build the "surface menu step for step".
20. **scenes.md 12 (game over).** The letters' individual positions, whether they animate during the 240 frames, and the
    palette are not given (18 tiles, x 99-166, y 59-106). Whether button input is accepted during the animation, and any
    lock-out on the last-password screen before a button restarts, is not stated.
21. **Transition between scenes.** Fade-in/out, blank frames and the 22 + 13 frame load (game-flow 1.1) are mentioned in
    passing; scenes.md has no per-scene entry/exit rule (screen blanked? palette fade?). Add a column.
